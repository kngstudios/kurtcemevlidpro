Privacy Policy

Kürtçe Mevlid Pro

Developer: KNG Studio  |  Package ID: com.kngstudio.mevlid
Effective Date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KNG Studio (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information in connection with the Kürtçe Mevlid Pro mobile application (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

The App is designed as an offline-friendly religious text reader and does not require user registration, account creation, or login. We do not operate our own servers to collect, store, or sell your personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a smooth reading experience, the App stores the following preferences and reading data strictly locally on your device using Android DataStore:

  • Your last read chapter (Bâb) and page number (to resume where you left off)
  • Reader display settings (font size, selected reading theme, and translation/meaning visibility)
  • Saved page bookmarks
  • Your personal Salavat counter value

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. You can delete bookmarks or reset the Salavat counter inside the App at any time, or remove all locally stored data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner advertisements. Even though we do not collect personal data ourselves, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information in order to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and improve ad performance.

Information processed by Google AdMob may include:

  • Advertising ID: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) or other device identifiers
  • Approximate Location: General region or country inferred from your IP address (the App never accesses precise GPS location)
  • Device & App Information: Device model, operating system version, screen size, language settings, and app version
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad impressions, clicks, crash logs, and performance diagnostics
For more details on how Google collects and processes data, please review:
• Google Privacy Policy
• How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
• Google AdMob & Privacy

4. Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time through your Android device settings (typically under Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads depending on your device) by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID, or via Google Ads Settings.

5. App Permissions

The App requests only the minimal permissions required to display banner ads:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to check network availability and load banner advertisements when you are online.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and measurement purposes.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, files/storage, or precise location.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party services in the App, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

7. Data Security

All bundled religious texts inside the App are protected against unauthorized extraction, and all local user preferences remain inside Android's sandboxed application storage. Network communications conducted by Google AdMob use encrypted HTTPS connections.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, third-party SDKs, or App features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date.”
